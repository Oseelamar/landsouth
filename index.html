Our services

Serving Lakeland property owners since 1978

Land South Properties and Management is one of Lakeland's largest and most diverse property management companies, serving investment property owners in the region since 1978.

  1. Marketing

    We'll prepare your property for rent and evaluate the current rental market to get you the best return on your investment, then market it on a host of platforms.

  2. Screening & selection

    A stringent screening process ensures your property is filled with reliable, responsible tenants.

  3. Maintenance & inspections

    Your property is a significant investment. We see to maintenance requests immediately and carry out regular inspections.

  4. Tenant move-out

    We take care of every move-out step, including final inspections, security deposit returns and turnover maintenance.

Switching to Land South

How switching works

Step 01

Free rental analysis

Send us your property address. We'll study the Lakeland rental market and tell you what your home should rent for.

Step 02

Easy onboarding

We take over the paperwork: leases, deposits, keys and introductions to any current residents. You don't have to chase your old manager.

Step 03

Your first rent paid

Rent is collected online and paid out to you, with every statement waiting in your owner portal.
